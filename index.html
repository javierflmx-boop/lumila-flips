The Reality

This isn't a weekend show.

01

What's behind the walls

You don't know what a house actually needs until demo starts. Old wiring, plumbing, or foundation issues show up after the offer is already accepted — not before.

02

Permits don't move on TV time

A single failed inspection can stall a project for weeks. Every permit pulled and inspection passed is operational reality, not a 45-minute episode.

03

Timelines slip — that's normal

Subcontractors miss dates. Materials get backordered. Every project has a target date and a buffer — slippage gets managed, not ignored, and it's priced in from the start.

04

The market doesn't wait for you

Every month a house sits unsold costs money — the loan, the taxes, the insurance. Timing the sale matters as much as the renovation itself.

This is why the operator matters more than the money. 20 years of doing this is what keeps a flip on schedule and on budget — not luck.
